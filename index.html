Maintenance Planning Tool Help
Aircraft Maintenance Planner — User Guide
Getting Started — Uploading a Due List

Maintenance Planning Tool processes PDF due list reports exported from CAMP Systems or MyCMP. To begin:

  1. Drag and drop your due list PDF onto the drop zone on the Home page, or click the drop zone to browse for the file.
  2. Maintenance Planning Tool will automatically detect the report format (CAMP or MyCMP), parse all maintenance items, and display the results.
  3. Reports up to 100 pages are supported. All processing happens locally in your browser — no data is uploaded anywhere.
Aircraft Utilization

After uploading a due list, the Aircraft Utilization ribbon appears above the maintenance events. Set your aircraft's typical monthly usage:

  • HRS / month — average flight hours per month (0–60)
  • AFL/ENC / month — average landings or engine cycles per month (0–60)

These rates are used to project maintenance event start dates on the Gantt timeline. Adjusting them updates all projected dates instantly.

Maintenance Events

Maintenance Planning Tool groups your due items into up to three maintenance events based on how soon they are due:

  • Event 1 — items due within approximately 180 days
  • Event 2 — items due between 180 and 365 days
  • Event 3 — items due beyond 365 days

Each event card on the Home page shows a summary of the items included, and lets you download a PDF or XLS report for that event. The Download all PDFs and Download all as XLS buttons generate reports for all active events at once.

Short Items

The Short Items section highlights maintenance items that fall within a critical threshold — items due very soon that may require immediate attention.

  • Default thresholds: ≤ 7 days, ≤ 15 flight hours, ≤ 15 AFL/ENC
  • Tolerance values are included — an item qualifies if its remaining value plus any published tolerance falls within the threshold. This means items whose hard due date is close, even if tolerance keeps them technically current, will still appear as short.
  • AFL/ENC values do not carry tolerance in CAMP/MyCMP reports and are compared against the threshold directly.
  • Adjust the thresholds on the Short Items tab to customize what counts as "short."

A short-items alert also appears on the Home page for quick visibility.

Edit Due Lists Tab

The Edit Due Lists tab shows all parsed maintenance items organized by event. Here you can:

  • Review remaining values (days/months, hours, AFL) and tolerance chips for each item.
  • Exclude specific items from an event by unchecking them — excluded items will not appear in exported reports.
  • Review the Overflow tab for any items that could not be assigned to a specific event.
  • Use the search/filter bar to quickly find items by reference number, description, or ATA chapter.
Timeline (Gantt Chart)

The Timeline tab displays an 18-month Gantt chart showing when each maintenance event is projected to begin and end based on utilization rates and proposed dates.

  • The vertical Today line shows the current date (report "as of" date).
  • Each bar represents the estimated downtime for that event. The projected start date is shown below the left edge of each bar.
  • Use the START DATE controls (←Wk / ←Day / Day→ / Wk→) on each bar to shift the event start date backward or forward. Use the DURATION controls (−Wk / −Day / +Day / +Wk) to shorten or extend the downtime window. A Reset button appears whenever adjustments are active.
  • Click Download PDF to export a professional timeline report, or Download XLS for a spreadsheet version.
Exporting Reports

Maintenance Planning Tool can export reports in two formats:

  • PDF — formatted maintenance event report including item list, tolerances, and event summary. Available per-event or all at once.
  • XLS / Excel — spreadsheet with all items for an event, useful for further analysis or sharing with maintenance providers.
  • Timeline PDF — the Gantt chart with event breakdown cards, suitable for planning meetings.
  • Timeline XLS — weekly grid spreadsheet mirroring the on-screen Gantt chart.
Compatibility Notes
  • Maintenance Planning Tool supports CAMP DueList PDF reports and MyCMP due list exports.
  • Multi-interval items (items with multiple inspection intervals) are handled automatically — the earliest-due interval is used for event assignment.
  • Items with calendar remaining values show DAYS or MOS (months) chips depending on how CAMP reports them.
  • Maintenance Planning Tool is an independent tool and is not affiliated with CAMP Systems International or MyCMP.
